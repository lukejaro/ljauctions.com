# Benefit auctioneer sites

Compared in October 2026. These are public marketing sites for auctioneers who call benefit and fundraising auctions. Nothing here is a claim about LJ Auctions.

## Comparison

| Site | Standout strengths | Mobile notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kurt Johnson Auctioneering](https://kurtjohnsonauctioneering.com/) | Long tenure, a large auction count, and a dollar total up front. Pre-sale, sale-day, and post-sale steps. Testimonials and team bios. Professional association marks. | Long page. Stats and a phone path are easy to find. |
| [David Goodman / Auction Results](https://www.auction-results.org/) | A short promise in the headline, a large fundraising total, named testimonials, and a phone call to action. | Phone number is prominent. The page is text-heavy. |

| [Murad Auctions](https://muradauctions.com/) | A recent fundraising total, live and silent options, a free consultation, and a phone number in the header. | Header phone is usable on a small screen. |

## Patterns worth matching

These do not need new facts:

- A hero that says what the auctioneer is for, with one primary action to check a date and a phone number beside it.
- A sticky call and book bar on small screens.
- A short stats band using only confirmed numbers.
- Benefit and charity auctions listed as their own service, next to estates and equipment.
- A three-step booking path and a short FAQ, with FAQ structured data.
- Real photographs of the auctioneer, with no event or venue names.
- A row of organizations he has actually worked for, using their own marks or a plain name when a mark is not available.
